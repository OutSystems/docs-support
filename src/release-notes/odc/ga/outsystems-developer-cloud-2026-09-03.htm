OutSystems Developer Cloud 2026-09-03

Released on Sep 03, 2026

New

  • The Application Health score no longer factors in errors or response times from Timers, so apps running background jobs overnight won't see their health score drop overnight and recover during the day. Timer metrics still show up in the rest of the analytics dashboard. This only affects health scores calculated from now on, past scores aren't recalculated. (RDASS-4038)
