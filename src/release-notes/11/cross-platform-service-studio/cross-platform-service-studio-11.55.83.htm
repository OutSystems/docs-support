Cross-platform Service Studio 11.55.83

This is a preliminary version of the document.

New in Cross-platform Service Studio 11.55.83

  • Notifications now surface inside the Studio. A Notification Hub in the top bar collects all your latest alerts and lists them with quick actions, while transient toasts announce new ones as they arrive. (RDEV-9895)
  • It is now possible to keep several Find Usages searches open at the same time, each in its own tab in the results panel, so you can follow a chain of usages without losing your earlier results. You can switch back to any previous search at any time, and expand an entry within a result to find its own usages in place. (RDEV-9897)

Bug Fixing

  • Fixed an issue where the preview data panel had a cut column. (RDEV-10057)
  • Fixed an issue that caused an 'Insecure connection' warning to appear during login due to certificates missing OCSP/CRL information. (RPM-1691) Service Studio
