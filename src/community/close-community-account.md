# How to close your OutSystems community account

Your OutSystems community account is the single login for the OutSystems Community, [ODC Personal Edition](https://www.outsystems.com/personaledition/), and [O11 Personal Environment](https://www.outsystems.com/Portal/Trial_Portal). You can close it at any time.

Closing your account is permanent. You lose all your profile data, private messages, Personal Edition, and Personal Environment.

To close your account, follow these steps:

1. Log in to the OutSystems website with your community account.

    **Note:** If you forgot your password, select [Forgot password?](https://www.outsystems.com/home/RequestPassword.aspx) on the login page.

1. In your profile menu, select **Settings**.

    ![Screenshot of the OutSystems community profile menu with the Settings option](images/close-account-profile-menu-pl.png "Profile Menu Settings Option")

1. Select the **More options** icon, then select **Close account**.

    ![Screenshot of the account settings page with the More options menu open and the Close account option](images/close-account-settings-pl.png "Close Account Option")

1. Select **Send code**, then check your email for the verification code.

    ![Screenshot of the Close account dialog with the Send code button](images/close-account-send-code-pl.png "Send Code Button")

1. Enter the verification code, then select **Close account**.

    ![Screenshot of the Close account dialog with the verification code field and the Close account button](images/close-account-close-account-pl.png "Verification Code Entry")

1. In the confirmation dialog, select **Close account**.

    ![Screenshot of the confirmation dialog with the Keep account and Close account buttons](images/close-account-close-account-confirmation-pl.png "Close Account Confirmation Dialog")

    You can't reactivate your account after you close it. You're logged out, and you receive an email confirming that your account is closed.
