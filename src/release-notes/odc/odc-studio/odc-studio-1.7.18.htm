ODC Studio 1.7.18

Released on Aug 03, 2026

New in ODC Studio 1.7.18

  • Mentor is visible by default in ODC Studio, and remembers whether you last kept it open or closed as you move across your apps and libraries. (RDEV-10007)
  • Upgraded node.js to version 24.17.0 to prevent known vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-48618 and CVE-2026-48933 (RDEV-10061)
  • The ODC Studio sign-in and sign-out screens now have a cleaner, more consistent visual design. (RDEV-9837)
  • Notifications now surface inside the Studio. A Notification Hub in the top bar collects all your latest alerts and lists them with quick actions, while transient toasts announce new ones as they arrive. (RDEV-9895)

Bug Fixing

  • Fixed an issue where the Splitter was not in the correct position in the Merge dialog. (RDEV-10054)
  • Fixed an issue that caused an unexpected error when refreshing dependencies in apps with a handler for a referenced Global Event. (RDEV-9987)
