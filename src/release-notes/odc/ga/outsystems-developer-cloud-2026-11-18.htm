OutSystems Developer Cloud 2026-11-18

Released on Nov 18, 2026

New

  • Data Fabric connections now recover correctly after rare transient infrastructure failures. As a result, low-code applications and timers are less likely to remain affected by temporary upstream outages. (RDDFT-295)
  • You can now group your assets and the people who build them into Teams, where each member's role in the team adds to their permissions on every asset the team owns. Teams are managed in the ODC Portal or through the User and Access Management API by users with the new "Manage teams" permission. See Teams and asset ownership for details. (RPOR-30629)
